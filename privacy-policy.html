Privacy Policy

Kamusta — a family check-in app
Last updated: 4 November 2026

Kamusta asks one question a day — "Kamusta?" — and you tap one button to tell your family you're okay. This policy explains what we hold, why, who sees it, and how to delete it.

Kamusta is not an emergency, safety, medical, or monitoring service. It does not summon help or guarantee anyone will notice if you don't check in. In an emergency, call 911.

1. Who is responsible

Kamusta is operated by Rica R. Redonia, a registered professional in the Philippines, who is the Personal Information Controller under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Contact: unremarkableapps@gmail.com — for any question or request in this policy. A postal address is available on request.

Some of what we hold can say something about a person's wellbeing — a missed check-in for someone in Alaga mode, or pressing "I need someone". We don't analyse it, never sell it, and show it only to that person's own family circle.

3. Who can see it

Your family circle. Other members of a circle you have joined see your first name, whether and when you checked in, how long you've been quiet, whether Alaga mode is on, whether you asked for someone, and your birthday if you added one. They cannot see your PIN. People can only join using your 6-digit family code — share it only with people you want in your circle.

Our service provider. Google Firebase runs the app's sign-in, database and notifications, processing data on our instructions only. Our database is in Singapore (asia-southeast1), chosen for speed in the Philippines — so your information is transferred outside the country. We remain accountable for it under the Data Privacy Act wherever it is held, and the transfer is covered by our data processing agreement with Google.

Nobody else. We do not sell or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, insurers or lenders. We would disclose it only if required by a valid order under Philippine law.

4. Why we may hold it

We rely on your consent — you choose to create or join a circle, turn on Alaga mode, allow notifications and add a birthday, and each can be withdrawn separately — and on performing our agreement with you, since a check-in app cannot work without recording check-ins.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, so your family can see your history.
  • When you delete your account, your records are removed immediately and cannot be recovered from within the app. Short-lived technical copies may remain briefly in our database provider's systems before being overwritten.
  • Circles empty for 7 days are deleted automatically.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to correct it, to object to its use, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to be compensated for damage caused by mishandling.

Most of this you can do yourself: change or clear your name and birthday in Settings, turn notifications off in your phone's settings, switch Alaga mode off (only you can), leave a circle, or delete your account. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days, free of charge.

Deleting everything: in the app, Settings → Delete my account, then type DELETE. This immediately erases your account, your membership of every circle, your check-in history, your birthday and your notification token. It cannot be undone; your family's own check-ins stay with them. If you no longer have the app, follow our data deletion page — we verify against our own records and tell no one else in your family that you asked.

7. Children

Kamusta is for adults. An adult creates a family circle and invites the people in it, and we do not direct the app at children or knowingly collect a child's information on our own initiative.

A family circle may nonetheless include a young person, because families include young people. In that case the adult who set up the circle is responsible for having the authority to share that information — under the Data Privacy Act, consent for a minor is given by a parent or legal guardian — and for explaining to the young person what the app does. We hold nothing about a child beyond a first name and, if the family chooses, a birthday with no year.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's information is in Kamusta without your consent, email us and we will delete it.

8. Keeping it safe

  • Everything between your phone and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • PINs are stored using scrypt hashing with a unique salt. We cannot read your PIN, and neither could anyone who obtained our database.
  • The app cannot write to the database directly — every change passes through our own server-side checks, so one person cannot alter another's records.
  • Repeated wrong PIN attempts are rate-limited.
  • If someone asks your family to reset your PIN, you are notified and have an hour to cancel it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affected your information we would notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.
