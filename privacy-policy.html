Privacy Policy

Kamusta, a family check-in app by unremarkableapps.
Last updated: 31 July 2026

Kamusta is a simple family check-in app. Once a day it asks "Kamusta?" and you tap one button to tell your family you're okay. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we hold, why, who can see it, and how you can get rid of it.

Kamusta is not an emergency, safety, medical, or monitoring service. It does not summon help, contact emergency services, or guarantee that anyone will notice if you do not check in. In an emergency, call 911.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Kamusta is operated by unremarkableapps, an individual developer based in the Philippines. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), we act as the Personal Information Controller for the information described below.

Contact: unremarkableapps@gmail.com

2. What we collect

We collect as little as we can. You do not need an email address, a phone number, or a social media account to use Kamusta.

What we never collect

  • Your location — Kamusta never asks for or records where you are
  • Your contacts, photos, files, messages, or call history
  • Your health records, medical information, or fitness data
  • Your payment card details — subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play, which never gives us your card information
  • Anything about what you do in other apps or on the web

A note about sensitive information

Some of what Kamusta records can say something about a person's wellbeing — for example, a missed check-in for someone in Alaga mode, or pressing the "I need someone" button. We treat this information with care, we do not analyse it, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. It is shown only to the members of that person's own family circle.

3. Who can see your information

Your family circle

This is the point of the app. Other members of a circle you have joined can see your first name, whether you have checked in today and when, how many days you have been quiet, whether Alaga mode is on for you, whether you have asked for someone, and your birthday if you have chosen to add one. They cannot see your PIN.

People can only join your circle if they have your 6-digit family code. Share it only with people you want in your family circle.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) to run the app — for sign-in, the database, and notifications. Google processes this information on our behalf, under our instructions, and does not use it for its own purposes.

Where your information is stored: our database and servers are located in Singapore (Google's asia-southeast1 region), chosen so the app is fast for users in the Philippines. This means your information is transferred outside the Philippines. We remain accountable for it under the Data Privacy Act wherever it is held.

If you buy a subscription, Google Play handles the payment. We are told only which plan a circle is on — never your card details.

Nobody else

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, lenders, or anyone else. Kamusta contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We would only disclose information if we were legally required to by a valid order under Philippine law.

4. Why we are allowed to hold it (legal basis)

Under the Data Privacy Act, we rely on:

  • Your consent — you choose to create or join a circle, to turn on Alaga mode, to allow notifications, and to add a birthday. Each of these is a separate choice and each can be withdrawn.
  • Performing our agreement with you — we cannot run a check-in app without recording check-ins.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your information is kept while your account exists, so your family can see your check-in history.
  • When you delete your account, your personal records are removed immediately, and purged from backups within 30 days.
  • Circles that have been empty for 7 days are deleted automatically.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to correct it, to object to how it is used, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to be compensated for damage caused by mishandling.

Most of these you can exercise yourself inside the app: your name and birthday can be changed or cleared in Settings, notifications can be turned off in your phone's settings, Alaga mode can be switched off by you and only by you, and you can leave a circle or delete your account at any time.

For anything else, email unremarkableapps@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting everything

In the app: Settings → Delete my account, then type DELETE. This immediately erases your account, your membership of every circle, your check-in history, your birthday, and your notification token. It cannot be undone. Your family's own check-ins stay with them.

If you no longer have the app installed, follow the instructions at our data deletion page. We verify your request against our own records and we do not tell anyone else in your family that you made it.

7. Children and young people

Kamusta is designed for adults and is not directed at children. An adult sets up a family circle and invites the people in it.

Families being what they are, a circle may include a young person, and an adult may enter a child's first name when adding them. In that case the adult who set up the circle is responsible for having the right to share that information, and for explaining to the young person what the app does. We ask for nothing about a child beyond a first name and, if the family chooses, a birthday without a year.

If you believe a child's information is in Kamusta and it should not be, email us and we will remove it.

8. Keeping it safe

  • Everything sent between your phone and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • PINs are stored using scrypt hashing with a unique random salt for each person. We cannot read your PIN, and neither can anyone who obtained our database.
  • The app cannot change the database directly — every change goes through our own server-side checks, so one person cannot alter another's records.
  • Repeated wrong PIN attempts are rate-limited.
  • If someone asks your family to reset your PIN, you are notified and have an hour to cancel it before it takes effect.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affected your information, we would notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
