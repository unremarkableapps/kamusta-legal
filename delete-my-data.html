Delete your Kamusta account & data

Kamusta, published by Rica R. Redonia, is a family check-in app. It is published on Google Play as unremarkableapps_ph. You can delete your account and personal data at any time. This page explains how — including if you no longer have the app installed.

Fastest: delete inside the app

  • Open Kamusta and tap the settings icon (top right).
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm.

This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Don't have the app anymore?

Email unremarkableapps@gmail.com from any address, and include:

  • your 6-digit family code;
  • your first name, as it was registered in the app;
  • roughly when you joined — the month is enough.

We check those details against our own records. We do not contact anyone else in your family to confirm your request, and no one in your circle is told that you made it. Your data is deleted within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in identity) and login PIN.
  • Your membership in every family circle.
  • Your own check-in history.
  • Your name and notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your family's own check-ins and circle stay with them — only your personal records are removed.
  • We keep no location, contacts, photos, or payment card data at any time (payments are handled by the App Store / Google Play).
  • Short-lived technical copies may remain briefly in our database provider's systems before being overwritten.

Kamusta is a personal family check-in app and is not an emergency, safety, or medical service.
